1 · Getting started

Create your shop and log in

It takes about two minutes and works on any phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install.

  1. Open Create a shop and choose your type of business: shop, pharmacy, school, restaurant or delivery service.
  2. Type your shop name. You get your own link, for example tessapos.shop/kofi-mart.
  3. Enter your name, email and a password with letters and numbers, then create the shop. You are the owner.
  4. Next time, go to Log in and use your email and password. Staff can also open the shop link and log in with their name and password.

Tip: in your phone's browser menu choose Add to Home screen. The shop then opens like an app with one tap.

2 · Selling

Make a sale and give a receipt

Everything happens on the 🛒 Sell page.

  1. Tap Sell, then tap each product the customer is buying. Tap again to add one more. Use the search box to find a product quickly.
  2. Check the Current sale list. You can change a quantity, remove an item or give a discount.
  3. Choose how the customer pays: Cash MoMo Card or Credit. A sale can be split between two methods.
  4. Tap Charge. The receipt opens: tap 🖨️ Print, or 📱 SMS to text it to the customer.

Made a mistake? Open 🧾 Receipts, find the sale and choose refund. The stock goes back by itself.

3 · Stock

Add products and keep stock right

Stock goes down by itself with every sale. You only record what comes in.

  1. Open 📦 Stock and tap + Add product.
  2. Enter the name, cost price, selling price and how many you have. You can add a photo, a category and an expiry date.
  3. When new goods arrive, tap Restock beside the product and type how many came in.
  4. Products that are running out are marked ⚠ low so you know what to buy.

Buying from suppliers? Use 📥 Purchases to record an order, receive it into stock and track what you still owe the supplier.

4 · Customers & credit

Sell on credit and get paid

Keep every customer's balance in one place, and let the app remind them.

  1. Open 👥 Customers and add the customer's name and phone number.
  2. At the till, choose the customer, pick Credit as the payment and set a pay-by date (7, 14 or 30 days, or any date).
  3. When the customer pays, open their name in Customers and record the amount. The balance goes down.
  4. With SMS switched on, the customer gets a text with the amount and the pay-by date, and a reminder when it is due.

5 · Staff

Give each worker their own login

Never share your owner password. Each person gets their own name, password and role.

  1. Open 🔐 Staff and add the person's name, a first password and their role.
  2. Pick the role that fits: Manager, Accountant, Cashier, Stock keeper, Warehouse keeper or Delivery. Each role only sees the pages it needs.
  3. Use 🔑 Access to give or remove single pages for one person.
  4. The activity trail on the same page shows who did what and when: sales, refunds, price changes and logins.

Cashiers see only their own sales and cannot see customer records, cost prices or reports.

6 · Expenses & reports

See how the business is doing

Record what you spend, then read your profit in a few taps.

  1. Open 💸 Expenses and record each cost with its type: rent, salaries, transport, utilities and so on.
  2. Open 📊 Reports and choose the period: today, this week, this month or your own dates.
  3. Read sales, profit, best sellers, stock value, customers who owe and staff performance.
  4. Save any report as a PDF to keep or share.

7 · Online shop

Let customers order and pay online

Every shop gets its own online shop at tessapos.shop/your-shop/shop, showing your products, prices and live stock.

  1. Open 🌐 Online shop → Settings to open or close the shop for orders, and to switch pickup and delivery on or off.
  2. To take real payments, paste your Paystack secret key under Payments. Customers can then pay with Mobile Money or a card.
  3. Add photos to your products. Tick Hide from the online shop on any product you do not want to show.
  4. New orders appear under Orders. Mark each one ready, then collected or delivered.

SMS receipts and reminders use your own USMS-GH account. Paste its token and sender ID in Online shop → Settings.

8 · Your type of business

Extra tools for pharmacies, schools, restaurants and delivery services

The type you chose when you created the shop adds its own pages on top of everything above.

💊 Pharmacy

Batches and expiry dates for every drug.

  • Open a product and tap 🧪 Batches & expiry to add each batch with its expiry date.
  • The oldest batch is sold first, and expired stock cannot be sold.
  • 💊 Expiry watch lists what expires soon.
  • Mark prescription-only medicines on the product.

🎓 School

Students, fees, attendance and results on the School page.

  • Set the academic year, term, classes and subjects (a ready Ghana list is included).
  • Add students one by one, or paste a whole class list.
  • Bill a class, then 💵 Record a payment when fees are paid.
  • Take attendance, enter marks and print report cards.
  • Send announcements to parents by SMS.

🍽️ Restaurant

Tables, open bills and a kitchen screen.

  • Tap a table to open its bill and add what the guests order.
  • Orders go to the 👨‍🍳 Kitchen page. The kitchen taps Start cooking, then Ready.
  • Move a bill to another table or split it between guests.
  • Use 🥡 New takeaway or 🚚 New delivery order for orders with no table.
  • Print the bill and take payment when the guests finish.

📦 Delivery service

Parcel jobs, riders and cash on delivery on the Dispatch page.

  • Set your zones and prices once, and add your riders and regular clients.
  • Tap + New delivery, enter sender, receiver, item and any cash to collect.
  • Assign a rider. The rider marks Picked up, On my way and Delivered.
  • Record the cash riders hand in, and their payouts.
9 · Common problems

Quick answers

I forgot my password

On the Log in page tap Forgot password?, enter your email and we send you a 6-digit code. Type the code and choose a new password. Staff with no email on their account should ask the owner to reset it in Staff.

I can't remember my shop link

You don't need it. Go to Log in and use your email and password. We take you straight to your shop.

The page says "Offline" or "Not saved yet"

The app needs the internet to save your work. Check your data or Wi-Fi, and keep the page open: your changes are saved as soon as the connection is back. Do not close the page while it still says "Not saved yet".

My receipt will not print

Connect the printer to the phone or computer first (USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi). The first time you tap 🖨️ Print, choose your printer in the print window. In Settings → Receipt printing pick the right paper: 80 mm roll, 58 mm roll or A4. Use Print a test receipt to check.

A staff member is locked out

After several wrong passwords an account is locked for safety. The owner can unlock it and set a new password in Staff.

My stock number is wrong

Open the product in Stock and adjust the count, choosing the reason (damaged, expired, lost, correction). Every change is kept in the stock history so you can see what happened.

Is my data safe if my phone is lost?

Yes. Nothing is kept on the phone. Your shop is saved online and backed up automatically, so you can log in from any other device and carry on.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Open your shop on as many phones, tablets and computers as you like, at the same time. Everyone sees the same stock and sales.
