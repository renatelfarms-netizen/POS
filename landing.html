Everything a shop needs

Built and tested in a real shop with two branches and a warehouse.

Fast selling

Cash, MoMo, card or credit. Split payments, discounts and receipts for 58 mm, 80 mm or A4 printers.

Stock & warehouse

Low-stock alerts, purchases from suppliers, and transfers from the warehouse to each branch.

Staff & permissions

Owner, managers, cashiers and more. Each person logs in with their own name and password.

One or two branches

Start with one. Add a second branch in Settings whenever you're ready.

Reports by email

Daily and monthly reports as PDF, sent automatically. See profit, expenses and who sold what.

Safe online

Automatic backups every 10 minutes and a full activity trail. Nothing is lost when a phone is.

Start selling

Staff open your shop link, log in, and sell. You see everything live.
